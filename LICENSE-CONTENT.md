## Exceptions and third-party material

**Pencil drawings.** Each drawing is made from a plate on Wikimedia Commons. Its source, licence and attribution are recorded in `imagenes/<slug>/ficha.json` and listed in `NZ-A-fuentes`.
- **CC BY-SA (share-alike):** these three drawings stay under their source's licence.
  - `kiwifruit-karwath`: photo by André Karwath, CC BY-SA 2.5.
  - `kuaka-keulemans`: bar-tailed godwit, J. G. Keulemans in W. L. Buller (1888), scan by NZETC, CC BY-SA.
  - `hoiho-keulemans`: yellow-eyed penguin, same source, CC BY-SA.
- **CC BY:** `tuatara-wellcome`, a wood engraving from the Wellcome Collection. The drawing is CC BY 4.0 with attribution to the Wellcome Collection.
- **Public domain:** all the other plates. The pencil drawings made from them are CC BY 4.0.
