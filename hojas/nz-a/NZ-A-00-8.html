A short history

c. 1250–1300
Polynesian voyagers, the ancestors of Māori, settle the islands.
1642
Dutch navigator Abel Tasman sights the coast; after a clash in Golden Bay he leaves without landing.
1769
James Cook lands at Tūranganui-a-Kiwa (Gisborne) and goes on to map both islands.
1840
The Treaty of Waitangi is first signed at Waitangi, Bay of Islands, on 6 February: now Waitangi Day.
1893
The first self-governing country where all women can vote in national elections.
1953
Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay reach the top of Everest.
1987
Te reo Māori becomes an official language.
2011
The Christchurch earthquake of 22 February kills 185 people.
